# Shipping and delivery

Standard shipping within Australia takes 3 to 6 business days and is free on orders over $80. Below $80, standard shipping costs $9.95.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days to metro areas and costs $14.95.

We ship to New Zealand in 7 to 10 business days for $24.95. We do not currently ship to other countries.

Orders placed before 1pm AEST on a business day are dispatched the same day.

You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order is dispatched. If your tracking has not updated in 5 business days, contact us and we will open an investigation with the courier.
