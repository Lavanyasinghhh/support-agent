# Changing or cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge until it is dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

To add or remove items from an order that has not been dispatched, cancel it and place a new order.

Price adjustments: if an item drops in price within 7 days of your purchase, we will refund the difference. Contact support with your order number.
