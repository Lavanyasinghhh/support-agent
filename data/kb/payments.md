# Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Afterpay.

Afterpay splits your purchase into four fortnightly payments with no interest. Afterpay is available on orders between $35 and $2000.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. We never ask for your full card number by chat or email.

If a payment fails, check that your billing address matches the address your bank has on file, then try again.
