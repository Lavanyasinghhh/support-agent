# Returns and refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

To start a return, go to My Orders, choose the order and select "Return item". We email you a prepaid return label within one business day.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once the warehouse receives your return, the refund takes 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement.

Sale items marked "Final sale" cannot be returned. Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded.

Refunds over $200 are reviewed by a team member before they are approved.
